    Where the dates come from

    Every date on this page is quoted from the organizer's own website or the IFPA calendar. Each night a script reads the sites, and a date only goes up if the exact words are on the page. When we can't confirm something, we say "TBA" instead of guessing. Always confirm on the organizer's site before you buy anything.

    Run one of these events?

    Put your dates, registration time and time zone on your website in plain text, with the year. This page picks them up overnight. Dates that only live in Discord or Facebook can't be read.
