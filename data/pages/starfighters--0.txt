The Starfighters Story

We're Arizona's original retro arcade created by vintage arcade collectors and enthusiasts

Founded by two arcade collectors, the mission at Starfighters Arcade is to promote, preserve, and project the vintage arcade gaming experience into the future for new generations to discover and enjoy.

With this humble goal in mind, we devote the arcade to providing a once in a lifetime experience for Starfighters yound and old to become immersed in the sights, sounds and environment we remembered as teenagers growing up in the late 70’s and early 80’s.

Starfighters is a living museum with authentic artifacts spread throughout the arcade to complement the games we’ve rescued and restored over years to add to our collection and share with the valley community.

If it's time to reclaim that childhood feeling you had years ago when just spending time at the arcade with friends after some pizza was a great night out, Starfighters is the place for you. Come play your all-time favorite pinball machines, listen to great music, or just save the universe from marauding hordes of alien invaders and escape!!!

Retro Rules!

StarFighters Arcade honors a time when Saturday afternoon meant a stack of quarters and defending the universe against an alien invasion.

Discover the freedom, fun, and adventure of 80s gaming at StarFighters Arcade. We're a retro-arcade located in Mesa, AZ offering over 160 rotating vintage games and pinball machines. With unlimited play, you can experience a unique moment in history and culture–the 80s arcade.

We're more than just a place to play video games; we're an active social club bringing together enthusiasts for tournaments, parties, and concerts. Become a member to improve your high score on classic games and meet new and old friends who share your love of retro gaming and 80s culture.

Reclaim that childhood feeling of spending a Saturday in the arcade, and share it with your family. All ages are welcome, but an adult must accompany Starfighters under the age of eight.
