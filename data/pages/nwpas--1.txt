We throw the largest arcade party in the Northwest, join us for the fun June 4-6 at the 2027 Northwest Pinball & Arcade Show!

<—Check our our rad poster designed by Derek Yaniger!

What to expect:

A mammoth arcade of diverse games to play all weekend!
500+ games are free to play with admission, spread out over 40,000 feet of square space along with vendors and tournaments! You will play pinball and arcade games that are super rare, the newest games, homebrew DIY games, antique games, and all your favorites!

Guest Speakers!
Pinball and arcade game designers, makers, artists, programmers, sound engineers, DIY game makers, and more. They speak, sign stuff, will do selfies with you, chat, and hang out all weekend. And they don’t charge for autographs and selfies like at most local cons!

So much more!
Costume contest, kid’s events, a face painter, show merch, tech seminars, rare collections, pinball tournaments, vendors, movie night, and tons of other cool stuff. Come play, shop, and have fun! Play Stern’s newest pinball games, get a selfie with Roger Sharpe, play in a pinball tournament, sit in on a tech seminar or guest speaker, and meet the people that designed the newest Stern Pinball game Transformers!

At the Greater Tacoma Convention Center
1500 Commerce St, Tacoma, WA 98402 ‍

Friday, June 4, 2026: 12pm-11:30 pm
Saturday, June 5, 2026: 10am-11:30 pm
Sunday, June 6, 2026: 9am-3 pm

Tickets are for sale at the door!
