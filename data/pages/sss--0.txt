Welcome to The Pinball Capital

The Pinball Capital is a dedicated pinball venue just outside Chicago, built for players of all skill levels – from first-time flippers to seasoned competitors. Whether you’re here for league night, a weekend tournament, or just to play a few games, you’ll find a welcoming, inclusive space focused on great games and great community.

Our Pinball Games

The Pinball Capital was created as a safe, welcoming place for all races, genders, ages, and skill levels. Our diverse collection includes more than 130 pinball machines, with games ranging from the mid-1960s through present-day 2026 releases. Visitors can enjoy modern Stern pinball machines, beloved classics, and rare games that represent decades of pinball history. Our lineup offers something for every generation and every type of player – from nostalgic collectors to first-time guests discovering why pinball is such an exciting and timeless hobby.

We buy & sell games!

Yes – we buy pinball machines, collections, and unique games. We also collaborate with pinball arcades, nonprofit organizations, collectors, and community partners. If you are interested in selling a pinball machine, volunteering for The Pinball Capital events, partnering with us, or showcasing a limited-edition pinball machine, we would love to hear from you.

Join us

The Pinball Capital hosts weekly tournaments, prizes, giveaways, and community events – and we remain open to the public during tournament play. With more than 130 pinball machines available, guests can enjoy open play, discover new favorites, and experience pinball in a fun, welcoming environment. Whether you are competing, practicing, visiting with friends, or bringing the family, there is always something to enjoy at Pinball Capital.

Games for Your Bar / Profit Share

Interested in collaborating with The Pinball Capital? If you have an awesome space that could benefit from the excitement of a pinball machine, we would love to connect.
