INDISC Tournament Series – February 4-7, 2027indisc24admin <https://www.indisc.com/author/indisc24admin/> 2026-11-16T15:12:51-08:00

The INDISC Tournament Series™ is four full days of open competitive pinball play welcoming players of all ages and skill levels. INDISC™ hosts a total of 7 tournaments over the course of the 4 day event including The Open IFPA World Championship™—one of the largest pinball tournaments in the world and an IFPA recognized major. In addition, there are divisions for Classics (an official Barrels of Fun Pinburgh Tour event), Women’s, Youth, and Seniors. We also host the Classics Target Match Play Championship, currently the only tournament of it’s kind. Gottlieb Stakes will not continue in 2027 but keep an eye out for an announcement of next year’s non-IFPA side tournament.

The Open IFPA World Championship™ along with Classics Championship, Women’s Championship, and Youth are open to all eligible players at INDISC with a valid event pass. The Classics Target Match Play Championship tournament requires a separate entry fee in addition to an event pass.

Free Play Area

INDISC is open to both competitive and non-competitive players and maintains a bank of free play machines for anyone holding a valid event pass for the weekend to enjoy. If you’re accompanying someone who’s participating in the tournaments, or bringing along kids and family members, INDISC is designed to satisfy non-competitive pinball fans as well.

Location

Located in sunny Murrieta California at the Murrieta Hot Springs Resort <https://www.murrieta-hotsprings.com/> .

Murrieta Hot Springs Resort <https://www.google.com/maps/place/39405+Murrieta+Hot+Springs+Road+Murrieta+CA+92563>

39405 Murrieta Hot Springs Rd.

Lodging

INDISC recommends the Murrieta Hot Springs Resort for the best tournament experience.

For those wishing to stay offsite, there are no less than a dozen non-partnered hotels located within a few miles of the resort.

Travel

There are seven major airports accessible to Murrieta and the Murrieta Hot Springs Resort. Ontario (ONT) in the Inland Empire is the closest and is recommended for domestic travel due to proximity. For those traveling internationally, there are many more direct flights to LAX which can be less expensive but please be aware that the commute can regularly take 2 hours or more, especially during peak traffic times. Additionally, SAN currently has options to and from Europe via London (Heathrow) and Munich.

Ontario Int. Airport (ONT) – 48 miles

John Wayne/OC Airport (SNA) – 64 miles

Palm Springs Int. Airport (PSP) – 65 miles

San Diego Int. Airport (SAN) – 66 miles

Long Beach Airport (LGB) – 77 miles

Los Angeles Int. Airport (LAX) – 90 miles

Burbank Airport (BUR) – 102 miles

San Bernardino (SBD – 47 miles) has non-daily flights from SFO and PVU only.
