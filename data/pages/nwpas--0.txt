SCHEDULE

Northwest Pinball and Arcade Show (NWPAS) provides competitive pinball players in the Pacific Northwest a high quality pinball tournament experience, that begins with the satellite series, and ends with several major events at the show, including the prestigious NW Women's Pinball Championship, the Open Tournament, a Match play Classic event, a Rookie and Amateur tournament and a Sunday Revenge group knockout event.

​

This page provides a summary of the NWPAS tournament schedule and summary of each tournament at the show, Friday June 5 through Sunday June 7, 2026.

​

For information on the satellite tournaments held before the main event, Click Here <https://hisokajp.wixsite.com/nwpas/satellite-event>

-Open Tournament, 180 spots, $70 + ticketing site fees for registration and 32 entries, 8 mores can be bought as a rebuy for $15 up to 68 maximums onsite during the tournament (last rebuy is 12 entries).

​

-Northwest Women's Pinball Championship, 96 spots (split in 4 sessions), $30 + ticketing site fees to register for one qualifying round. No rebuy allowed this year.

​

-Classics Match Play Tournament, 168 spots (split in 3 sessions), $50 + ticketing site fees to register for one qualifying round. No rebuy allowed this.

​

-Rookie & Amateur Group Knockout Pinball Tournament (Matchplay ranking lower bound 1499 or below), 60 spots, $10 + ticketing site fees to register for the tournament.

No online registration for Revenge Group Knockout Pinball Tournament. First come first serve on Sunday morning.

No tournament entries will be awarded during the satellite events, only show weekend passes.

Every attempt is made to keep the rules and information on this website accurate and up-to-date, and to run tournaments per the advertised structure and schedule. However, due to the nature of pinball and the fact that the tournaments are entirely run by volunteers, the tournament officials reserve the right to modify the tournament structure or schedule until the start time of that tournament. Late or last minute changes will be announced as appropriate prior to the start of the tournament. Changes to the tournament structure will not be made mid-tournament unless circumstances make such a change absolutely necessary.

Prizes listed on this website are planned assuming the tournament meets minimum attendance goals that match prior year as well as sponsorship level toward tournament prizes. Prizes listed may be decreased if total attendance is lower for any reason, including severe weather or natural disaster, power outages, technical issues, or covid-19 related changes that may force cancellation of rounds or reduction in total number of entries in each event.
