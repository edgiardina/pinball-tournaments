Formats:

Friday Warm Up :

Warm-up tournament on Friday. It will be seven or eight rounds of 4-player groups. The best 8 or 16 players advance to playoff depending on number of players. Entry fee 20€ (100 player limit)

Rockpin, Finnish ECS:

Best Game 19 rock themed games, Top 32 Playoffs, 4 player group with 4 machines.

300% TGP, entry fee 30€ (128 player limit)
