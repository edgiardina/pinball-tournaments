Hungarian Pinball Open 2026

The Hungarian Pinball Open will be held again this year, just like in previous years, across three venues: Game Galaxy, Mabra <http://www.mabra.hu/en> , and the Pinball Museum, in cooperation with one another.

On this page, we aim to gather all useful information related to the tournament. Of course, feel free to contact us by email if you cannot find the answer to something here.

Registration starts at: 2026.July 06. 20:00 CET
