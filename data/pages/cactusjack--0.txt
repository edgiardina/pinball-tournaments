May 2nd-3rd, 2026

Cactus Jack’s Family Fun Center

Oklahoma City, Oklahoma

Located at Cactus Jack’s Family Fun Center, one of the Southwest’s premier pinball destinations. Cactus Jack’s Silverball Showdown is a 2 day match play tournament featuring competition on machines of all eras. Additionally, per-Showdown tournaments will be held on Thursday, April 30th and Friday, May 1st as well as the consolation tournament on Sunday. Players will be competing for cash prizes, trophies, and World Pinball Player Rankings points in this IFPA-Certified event.
