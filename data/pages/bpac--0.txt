What is BPAC?

The Brisbane Pinball & Arcade Collective (BPAC) is a five-day annual showcase celebrating competitive pinball, classic arcade gaming, and the community that surrounds them.

Drawing players from Australia and around the world, BPAC combines world-class competition with an inclusive, welcoming environment for newcomers and seasoned pros alike.
