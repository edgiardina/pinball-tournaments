Featuring

The 2026 show marks the fifteenth year of the Louisville Arcade Expo for an entire weekend full of retro excitement!

Do you remember arcades in shopping centers and malls filled with video games and pinball machines? Did you
ever play Adventure on the Atari 2600? How about making a greeting card with an Apple II and using Print
Shop? With hundreds of arcade and pinball machines on FREE PLAY and every console you can imagine,
the Louisville Arcade Expo is where you can relive all your memories. Best of all bring your
family and show them how good you are at some real gaming.

Pinball and Arcade Video Games

Lots of games from the 60's to today on free play, so leave your quarters at home!

Classic Consoles

Everything from Pong to games made at the beginning of this century!

Classic Computers

All the games you love on the systems that you grew up on.

Cosplay

Come dressed as your favorite characters and enter our costume contests.

Tournaments & Competitions

Test your skills in arcade, pinball, and console tournaments throughout the weekend.

2026 Special Guests

Samantha Kelly

The legendary voice actor of Princess Peach and Toad, Samantha Kelly, will be joining us for our 15th Expo!
She's been falling into boiling lava and dodging shells for the last decade.
Samantha Kelly is a voice actor and radio personality who has voiced Princess Peach and Toad since 2007.
She has been bringing characters to life in video games since 2003, including Sunny Day,
the sports announcer in the Backyard Sports series for kids.

Appearing all 3 days of LAX 2026 - Be sure to stop by and meet Samantha in person for
autographs, photos, and a chance to get your Peach and Toad games signed.
It's a perfect opportunity for Nintendo fans and collectors to meet one of the most iconic voices
in gaming history.

Kenny James

Kenny James will be appearing at LAX 2026!

Kenny is best known as the voice of Bowser in the Nintendo Super Mario Bros franchise with over 35 title credits.
He has been providing the voice and personality of Bowser since Super Mario Strikers in 2005.
Other voice work includes Zingo, a story telling dragon for a children's program, narration and audiobooks.

Swing by Kenny's booth to say hello, grab an autograph, snap a photo, and have your Bowser titles signed.
Whether you're a longtime Mario fan or adding to your collection, this is your chance to meet the voice
behind the King of the Koopas in person.

The 8-Bit Guy

The 8-Bit Guy <https://www.youtube.com/@The8BitGuy> (aka David Murray)
is a popular YouTube content creator focused on classic computers, vintage technology,
and arcade games. His channel explores the history, repair, and practical use of retro hardware,
with in-depth videos on systems like the Commodore, MS-DOS PCs, and classic arcade machines.
Known for his clear, hands-on explanations, David covers everything from hardware restorations
and technical deep dives to the real-world challenges of operating and maintaining an arcade,
making his content a must-watch for fans of retro computing and classic gaming.

Appearing all 3 days of LAX 2026 - David will be hanging out at his table and don't miss his panel on Friday evening!

John Riggs

John Riggs <https://linktr.ee/johnriggs> is a content creator
specializing in retro games and game hunting with over 34 million YouTube views.
From ranking classic games to unearthing hidden gems, hunting for games all over the US,
or showing how to fix broken games with Open Cart Surgery, John has something for everyone
that's into Retro gaming.

His newest game, Chew Chew Mimic, is available on Dreamcast and NES. Check out his nostalgic breakfast cereal book NostalgiaO's too!

Appearing all 3 days of LAX 2026 - John will be hanging out at his table and giving a panel, date/time to be determined.

Square Pegs

Jay Malone <https://www.youtube.com/@SquarePegs>
A former QA manager at Electronic Arts, Jay Malone is a retro gaming content creator based in Michigan,
and the host of Square Pegs on YouTube, where he spotlights classic games, forgotten franchises,
and modern titles with old-school energy. His work blends nostalgia, accessibility, and
thoughtful curation for longtime players and curious newcomers alike.

Appearing all 3 days of LAX 2026 - Jay will be hanging out at his table and giving a panel, date/time to be determined.

Retro Pub Trivia with The Trivia Nerd

Are you a Nintendo know-it-all? A sage of all things Sega? Ready to throwdown and take the trivia crown?

We have dozens of categories and questions awaiting you at the Retro Pub Quiz hosted by The Trivia Nerd!
Prizes for our top teams, and prizes for randomly drawn winners!

Gather your team and we'll see you at the Main Stage on Friday night at 8:00 pm!

Dr. Mario DrMC Kentucky Regional

Dr. Mario has been a beloved Nintendo puzzle classic game since its original release in 1990.
In 2017, the DrMC crowned the first Dr. Mario National Champion at ReplayFX in Pittsburgh, PA.
Now in its 10th year, the DrMC has expanded to a series of tournaments held across the United States.

We are looking for all Dr. Mario players, both skilled and novice, to join us at the
Louisville Arcade Expo to crown our fourth annual DrMC Kentucky Regional Champion!
The winner of the DrMC Kentucky Regional will get free admission to the 2026 DrMC Grand Championship, a cash prize and a killer trophy!

Just $10 to enter, tickets available at the Dr Mario tournament area. Admission to Louisville Arcade Expo required.

Signups begin at 10:00 AM Saturday. Finals begin at 7:00 PM Saturday.

More Info <https://docs.google.com/document/d/e/2PACX-1vQRSYo6gUsII2pQVEN7bf4etHfZlNNFLT9_I3xCPQAULGUEcFYDbsHOmgfxQzfrnaUyO4uejYgCcnY6/pub>

Mystery Game Fight Club

Mystery Game Fight Club is a tournament organizer local to the Louisville area.
Our specialty is competitions where every individual match is a different, randomly chosen game.
Our tournaments include: Mystery Fighting Game, Mystery Puzzle Game, and Mystery ANY Game, among others!

All weekend long we will have tournaments scheduled, as well as free play on the machine during down times and a dedicated Primal Rage arcade setup.
