Countdown to Pinburgh 2026

July 22-26

Pinburgh Returns for Year Three! After a triumphant relaunch in 2024, Pinburgh is back for its third year of rebirth – bigger, bolder, and buzzing with the energy of competitive pinball. Join us in celebrating the return of the world’s favorite match-play tournament, right here in the heart of Pittsburgh (well, sorta)!

Pinburgh 2026 isn’t just one epic tournament — it’s a five-day celebration of competitive pinball! Join us July 22-26, 2026, at Rezzanine Esports <https://www.rezzanineesports.com/> , where players will battle for over $60,000 in prizes. Alongside the main event, we’re thrilled to feature the Women’s International Pinball Tournament (WIPT™), the Bash at the Burgh, and Steel City Strikes — each bringing their own flavor of fun and fierce competition.

Be sure to follow PGH Pinball for the latest Pinburgh Announcements.
