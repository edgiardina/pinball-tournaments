About Us

District 82 Pinball Arcade is a family-friendly pinball arcade in De Pere, Wisconsin, just minutes from Green Bay. We’re a fun, welcoming place for kids, parents, and pinball fans of all ages to play together.

Our arcade features over 120 pinball machines plus over 40 classic arcade games, all on unlimited free play. Pay one simple entry fee and enjoy unlimited games all day — no quarters needed.

Located just 15 minutes from Lambeau Field, District 82 is an easy stop for families looking for something fun to do near Green Bay. Whether it’s your first time playing pinball or a family tradition, there’s always something new to enjoy.

Come flip, play, and make memories at District 82 Pinball Arcade.
