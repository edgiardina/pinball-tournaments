THE BEAST – July 8-11, 2027

It’s Year 5 of THE BEAST! July 8-11, 2027 at Pocketeer Billiards in Buffalo, NY.

Once again, proudly part of the Stern Pro Circuit in 2027 for the Main tournament

…AND also this year, it’s the final stop of the Barrels of Fun Pinburgh Tour for the Classics tournament!

THE BEAST will once again be held at Pocketeer Billiards, 2444 Clinton St., in Buffalo, NY and streamed LIVE on FoxCitiesPinball on Twitch.

Limited to 150 players. Weekend passes for THE BEAST are $110 for those who volunteer two 1-hour shifts of scorekeeping and $140 for non-volunteers. All volunteers who do two 1-hour shifts receive 7 free entries! Weekend passes go on sale Fri., Feb. 26th at 6pm ET online at www.pinballprofile.com/thebeast and also on THE BEAST Facebook event page and Discord channel. The confirmations are done manually to avoid expensive transaction fees for you! Player lists will be updated frequently. Payment (via Venmo/PayPal for US players or PayPal for outside USA). For those who pay before Friday, March 19th at 6pm ET, they will receive an early bird bonus of 14 FREE game entries (a $40 value)!
