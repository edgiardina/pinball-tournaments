AUSTRALIAN PINBALL OPEN CHAMPIONSHIPS

NOVEMBER 26TH - 29TH 2026

The fourth Australian Pinball Open Championships will be held at Club Tweed, Tweed Heads NSW.

The main event will run from the Friday morning to Saturday evening for qualifying, with the finals on Sunday Morning. The format is the same as the UK Open and INSIDC World Championships with a 5 game card qualify format and top 40 players going into the finals (depending on number of players).

There will also be a Classics event that will be run in a similar format to the main event with similar qualifying times as the main event.

Side events will be held on the Thursday evening, Friday evening, and Sunday afternoon. A separate Women’s Championships will be run concurrently, and prizes in the main event will be available for best Junior and Senior Qualifying scores.

These events are open to all who come along, whether a beginner of seasoned competitor. Cost starts at $20 to enter, with big cash prizes and trophies to be won. Four days of competitive pinball for players of all ages and skill levels.

A minimum 80% of entry fees to be returned as prize money/trophies.

Held in Tweed Heads on the southern border of the sunny Gold Coast.

Bring the family for a great holiday and some pinball fun!
