Tournament Format

Below is a rough schedule. Some minor things can change. Final format will be set after registration – based on the number of players.

Friday – Warmup:

Start at 16:00
We will play group matchplay with balanced settings and 7-4-2-0 scoring.
If you come a bit late and miss some rounds, you can still join the tournament. Just find the tournament desk and make them activate you.
Group matchplay until 22:00 (10-12 rounds are expected)
Finals for the top 9 – finished at around 01:00

TOP 9:
– 3 groups of 3 players playing 3 machines (7-3-0 scoring).
– Highest seed (automatic tiebreaker “Strength of opponents” in Matchplay will be used for seeding) in each group picks the arena bank (they will be created during the tournament).
– Players play in the order listed, then rotate by one spot each game (highest seed starts 1st game and is player 3 on game 3).
– The winner of each group advances.

Final round:
– 3 players, 3 machines (7-3-0 scoring)
– Highest seed chooses arena bank that has not been played yet.
– Players play in the order listed, then rotate by one spot each game (highest seed starts 1st game and is player 3 on game 3).

Saturday – Main Tournament (~300%):

09:00 – Venue opens
09:15 – 09:45 – Greetings, registrations (be on time)
09:45 – 10:00 – Registration is closed, Award ceremony, Tournament preparation
10:00 – 13:00 – Balanced group matchplay (3-4 players per machine)
13:00 – 14:00 – Lunch break
14:00 – 18:00 – We continue with group matchplay until 13 rounds are completed
18:00 – 20:00 – Quarterfinals: Top 16 players will be divided into 4 groups and play 4 pinball machines (7-4-2-0 scoring). Automatic tiebreaker “Strength of opponents” in Matchplay will be used for seeding.

From this point forward, machines for each group are picked by the players at the start of the round. Each player chooses one machine – lowest seed picks first.

Player order is also chosen by qualifying seed before each game (top seed picks player order first). On the game you have chosen, you pick last regardless of the seed.

20:00 – 22:00 – Semifinals: Top 8 players will be divided into 2 groups and play 4 pinball machines (7-4-2-0 scoring).
22:00 – 00:00 – Finals: 4 players will compete on 4 pinball machines, using a 7-4-2-0 scoring system.

The target is to achieve a 200% TGP value for the IFPA world ranking. Since this is the main Slovenian tournament and also counts for ECS (European Championship Series), it will receive an additional bonus for a total of 300% TGP value.

Sunday – Fair Strike Knockout:

09:00 – Venue opens
09:15 – 09:45 – Greetings, registrations (be on time)
09:45 – 10:00 – Registration is closed, Award ceremony, Tournament preparation
10:00 – 13:00 – Fair strike knockout with 7 lives
13:00 – 14:00 – Lunch break
14:00 – 16:00 – Fair strike knockout with 7 lives
16:00 – 19:00 – Finals for top 9

*Fair strike knockout: Each player will have 7 lives. In a group of 4 players, the strikes will be 0-1-1-2, and in a group of 3 players, it will be 0-1-2, in a 2 player group 0-2.

The goal is for most players to finish by approximately 16:00, and the finalists by 19:00.

TOP 9:
– 3 groups of 3 players playing 3 machines (7-3-0 scoring).
– Players carry forward any remaining lives as points to the semi final round.
– Highest seed (any ties for seeding will be broken with a 1 ball game) in each group picks the arena bank (they will be created during the tournament).
– Players play in the order listed, then rotate by one spot each game (highest seed starts 1st game and is player 3 on game 3).
– The winner of each group advances.

Final round:
– 3 players, 3 machines (7-3-0 scoring)
– Highest seed chooses arena bank that has not been played yet.
– Players play in the order listed, then rotate by one spot each game (highest seed starts 1st game and is player 3 on game 3).

Access and Parking Details

Biljardna Hiša Ljubljana – Slovenska is on the outskirts of Ljubljana’s city center, at “Bavarski dvor.” It is easily accessible on foot or by public transport, with the main railway station and the “Bavarski dvor” bus stop nearby. It’s just a 10-minute walk to the city center.

Access by car is slightly more challenging, but there are parking options available nearby. Street parking (if you find a spot) is payable 24/7 (2 €/h), but there is a cheaper nightly fee.

The closest parking garage is here: https://maps.app.goo.gl/oorPgjAFuMog58FTA <https://maps.app.goo.gl/oorPgjAFuMog58FTA>
Price list and more info:
https://parkiraj.si/en/parking-garages/pg-trdinova/ <https://parkiraj.si/en/parking-garages/pg-trdinova/>
– 1 hour – 2,30 €
– 1 day – 35 €

There are several P+R locations available on the edge of Ljubljana. You can park the car there and use the bus to the location. Usually there is a direct line.

We recommend leaving your car at your hotel and walking to the location.

Okvirni sistem tekmovanj:

Spodaj je okviren urnik. Nekatere manjše podrobnosti se lahko spremenijo. Končni format bo določen po zaključku registracij, glede na število igralcev.

Petek – Warm-up:

Začetek okoli 16:00.
Igrali bomo group matchplay z “balanced” nastavitvami. Udeleženci se lahko pridružijo kadarkoli.
Skupinski matchplay bo potekal do 22:00 (pričakujemo 10–12 krogov).
Finala za najboljših 9, zaključek predvidoma okoli 01:00.

Sobota – Main (~300%):

08:30 – Lokacija odpre vrata
09:00 – 09:45 – Pozdrav, prijave in podelitev (bodite točni)

13:00 – 14:00 – Odmor za kosilo
14:00 – 18:00 – Nadaljevanje group matchplay dokler ne dosežemo 13 rund.
18:00 – 20:00 – Četrtfinale: Najboljših 16 se razvrsti v 4 skupine in igrajo 4 fliperje (7-4-2-0).
20:00 – 22:00 – Polfinale: Najboljših 8 se razvrsti v 2 skupini in igrajo 4 fliperje (7-4-2-0).
22:00 – 00:00 – 4 tekmovalci igrajo 4 fliperje, točkovanje 7-4-2-0.

Cilj je 200% TGP vrednosti za svetovno lestvico. Ker je to glavni slovenski turnir in šteje tudi za ECS (European Championship Series), dobi še dodatek za končnih 300%.

Dostop in parkiranje

Biljardna Hiša Ljubljana – Slovenska je na robu centra mesta Ljubljane, na “Bavarskem dvoru”.

Boljše lokacije za dostop peš ali z javnim prevozom si tako ne bi mogli želeti.

Praktično zraven glavne železniške postaje, tik ob avtobusni postaji “Bavarski dvor” in 10 minut peš do čistega centra mesta.

Dostopnost z avtom je malo slabša, vendar so še vedno na voljo parkirišča v bližini. Parkiranje na ulici (če najdeš mesto) je plačljivo od 6h do 22h (1,70 €/h).

Predlagamo, da pustite avto pri svojem hotelu in se do lokacije odpravite peš.
